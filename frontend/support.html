FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AtlasPi?

AtlasPi is a platform designed to improve merchant discovery, profile visibility, and useful Pi-oriented ecosystem growth.

Is AtlasPi an official Pi Core Team product?

No. AtlasPi is an independent project built to support practical discovery and trust inside the Pi ecosystem.

Does AtlasPi use real mainnet payments on support pages?

No. Support-related payment flows are clearly presented as testnet-only where applicable.

Who can use AtlasPi?

AtlasPi is being built for pioneers, merchants, and users who want stronger discovery and clearer ecosystem participation.
